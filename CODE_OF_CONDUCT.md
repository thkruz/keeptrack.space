# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Don't be a jerk. We are here to track satellites and write software, not to argue
about anything else. If you keep that in mind, the rest of this document will never
come up.

## What this project is about

KeepTrack is a technical project. Issues, pull requests, and discussions are for
orbital mechanics, rendering, data quality, and code. Your politics, religion, and
personal beliefs are your own business, and this repository is not the place to
promote or debate them.

That cuts both ways. Nobody here should be asked about, judged on, or excluded for
who they are, where they are from, what they believe, or how much experience they
have. We care whether your code is correct and whether you are reasonable to work
with.

## What is expected

- Argue about the code, not the person who wrote it. "This allocates every frame"
  is useful. "You clearly have no idea what you are doing" is not.
- Take criticism of your own work the same way. If someone finds a bug in your
  patch, that is the process working.
- Assume the other person is competent and busy. Most friction in open source is a
  missing sentence of context, not malice.
- When you are wrong, say so, fix it, and move on. Nobody is keeping score.
- Remember that most contributors are volunteers and not everyone here writes
  English as a first language.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, slurs, or attacks aimed at a person rather than their work.
- Publishing someone's private information (address, employer, email, real name)
  without their permission.
- Dragging unrelated political or ideological fights into the project.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, spamming, or ignoring
  maintainer decisions until people give up on you.

Strong technical opinions are welcome. Being relentlessly unpleasant while holding
them is not.

## Where this applies

Anywhere the project happens: this repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
Discord, and any place you are representing KeepTrack in public. It also applies to
private contact with other contributors that started here.

## Reporting

Email <admin@keeptrack.space>. Include links or screenshots if you have them.

Reports are read by the maintainer, handled privately, and the reporter's identity
is not shared. You will not be penalized for reporting something in good faith, and
you do not need to be certain it is a violation before you send it.

## What happens next

There is no formal tribunal here. In rough order, depending on what happened and
whether it keeps happening:

1. A private note explaining what was out of line.
2. A warning, plus a request to stay away from the people involved.
3. A temporary ban from the project's spaces.
4. A permanent ban.

Serious incidents skip straight to the end. Off-topic comments, heated threads, and
similar noise may simply be edited, locked, or deleted without any of the above.
The maintainer has the final say.
